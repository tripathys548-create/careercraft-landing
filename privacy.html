This Privacy Policy explains what data CareerCraft (operated by [Legal entity name]) collects when you use our website, browser extension, and related services, and how that data is used and stored.

1. What We Collect

DataSourcePurpose
LinkedIn headline, About text, experience bullets, education, skills Read from your own LinkedIn profile page by the browser extension, client-side Sent to our backend to generate AI rewrite suggestions and a matching resume
LinkedIn public ID (the "in/username" part of your profile URL) Extracted from the page URL by the extension Bind your license key to your LinkedIn account so it can't be reused elsewhere
Email address Provided to Razorpay at checkout Deliver your license key and purchase receipt
Payment details (card/UPI/netbanking) Entered directly into Razorpay's checkout Process payment. We never see or store your full card/UPI details — Razorpay handles this.
Support messages Submitted by you via the extension's support form Respond to your question or issue

We do not collect your LinkedIn password. The extension only reads text that is already visible on the page while you are signed into LinkedIn in your own browser — it does not access your LinkedIn login credentials at any point.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate AI-rewritten profile content and resume PDFs via our backend and our AI model provider.
  • To validate and enforce one license key per LinkedIn account.
  • To send your license key and transactional emails (e.g., purchase confirmation).
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To monitor for abuse (e.g., rate-limiting excessive requests from one IP or key).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train third-party AI models beyond the single request needed to generate your rewrite.

3. Third Parties We Use

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policy and terms.

4. Data Retention

We retain license key records and generation logs (the profile text you submitted and the AI output) for as long as your license key remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward for support and fraud-prevention purposes. You can request deletion of your data at any time — see Section 6.

5. Data Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. License keys are validated server-side before any profile data is processed. Access to production data and secrets is restricted to the operators of CareerCraft.

6. Your Rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or request that we delete it, by emailing support@webelvate.com with the email address or license key associated with your purchase. We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

CareerCraft is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email support@webelvate.com.
